The Goal

A mystery monster from the D&D 5.2.1 System Reference Document is hiding in the dungeon. Identify it in 8 guesses or fewer. A new monster is chosen each day, and you can start a random one any time with the Random monster button.

Making a Guess

  1. Start typing a monster's name. Suggestions appear as you type; use the mouse or the arrow keys and Enter to pick one.
  2. Press Guess. Only monsters from the list are accepted, and you can't repeat a guess.
  3. Your guess is compared with the mystery monster across five clues.
  4. Use the clues to narrow it down. The portrait at the top also becomes clearer with every guess.

Reading the Clues

Each tile is colored by how close you are:

  • Green: exact match
  • Gold: partly right
  • Red: wrong
Type
The creature type (Beast, Dragon, Undead, Fiend...). Green if it's the same, red if not.
CR
Challenge Rating, a measure of how dangerous the monster is. An arrow tells you whether the answer's CR is higher (↑) or lower (↓) than your guess. Fractions count: 1/8 < 1/4 < 1/2 < 1.
Habitat
Where the monster lives. Some monsters live in several habitats. Green if all of them match, gold if you share at least one, red if none.
Alignment
Moral outlook, from Lawful Good to Chaotic Evil, or Unaligned for mindless creatures and animals. Green is exact; gold means you share either the law/chaos axis or the good/evil axis; red means neither.
HP AVG
Average hit points. An arrow shows whether the answer has more (↑) or fewer (↓) HP than your guess.

Example

You guess Wolf and get: Type Beast, CR 1/4 ↑, Habitat Forest, Alignment Unaligned, HP 11 ↑. The monster is a beast with no alignment that is tougher than a wolf, so perhaps a Tiger or a Brown Bear is next.

Tips

  • Open with a monster that has a common type and middling stats to learn as much as possible.
  • There are many dragons, and each color shares a habitat and alignment with its own kind. A single dragon guess can tell you a lot about the rest.
  • Colors and arrows don't stack across rows for you: scan the whole board each time and combine the clues yourself.
